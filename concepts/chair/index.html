“Can you take a look at my store?”

My first move in Help Scout was to ask for the store link. There was a lot I could learn from the same view the shopper had.

02 / Storefront inspection

Before I asked for access, I opened the store.

I followed the shopper’s steps. If an app introduced the problem, I could identify it and explain what to send its developer. A theme issue took me down a different path.

03 / Theme escalations

Then the messages started coming from my teammates.

Support arranged access and sent me the reproduction steps in Trello. I worked on a duplicate and handed the preview back so the merchant could try it.

04 / Theme development

The sprint board filled up. My inbox stayed open.

I built sections from Figma, took fixes through testing and lead review, and kept handling escalations. Later I contributed to Kitenzo and returned to themes for modernisation and peer QA.
