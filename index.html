01 Support

Start with the storefront.

My first job at Eight was answering merchants in Help Scout. When someone sent a store link, I opened it and tried to see what they were seeing. If they hadn’t included one, that was my first question.

Help Scout · Storefront inspection
Read my workflow

Once I had the link, I could reproduce the problem and inspect the storefront before asking for access.

If an app introduced the problem, I identified it and explained what to share with its developer. For a theme bug, I requested access to make the repair.

I applied a known fix when one existed. A new app problem went to the development team with what I’d found.

02 Escalations

Give the merchant a safe preview.

In theme escalations, support requested access and sent me a Trello ticket with the store, symptoms and steps to reproduce. My job was to diagnose it and give the merchant a fix they could try.

Trello · Liquid · Notion · GitHub
Read my workflow

I duplicated the merchant’s theme and edited the copy. Support sent the merchant its name so they could preview the change before deciding whether to publish.

The ticket came back to me. I worked on the same duplicate and sent it through support for another check. The merchant controlled when it went live.

I wrote a step-by-step “dev tutorial” in Notion that a non-technical teammate could follow. I also filed a GitHub issue so the team could fix the theme for future installs.

03 Development

Take a section from Figma to review.

About six months to a year in, I joined the theme developers. My sprint work ranged from small fixes and new sections to redesigns, features and whole theme builds. I stayed on as escalation lead and helped onboard new developers.

GitHub Projects · Git · Figma · HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Liquid
Read my workflow

Product shaped the idea, design supplied the Figma file, and our project manager coordinated delivery. I picked up the ticket and built against both the design and the behaviour in the spec.

I picked up the ticket again, fixed the issue and sent it back through testing. I balanced these iterations with incoming merchant escalations.

Our lead developer reviewed the pull request for code quality. That review was the final pass in our delivery process.

04 Apps

See Bundle Builder from the other side.

When Eight shifted its focus to apps, our themes team learned React and app development. We each built an unpublished practice app from scratch. I then spent a few months contributing to Kitenzo, the product I’d once supported.

React foundations · Kitenzo · Sprint delivery
Read my workflow

Building the practice app gave me hands-on experience with React and the structure of a Shopify app. It was a learning project and stayed internal.

I implemented sprint tickets under the apps team’s lead developer. The tester checked the work and the lead reviewed the pull requests.

05 Modernisation

Own the checks as well as the change.

Back on themes, the theme lead and I updated existing products for newer technology and changing design expectations. For several months we also tested our own work and each other’s changes.

Peer QA · Claude Code · Code review
Read my workflow

We tested each other’s work as well as our own. I brought the same habit I’d learned in support: open the storefront and follow the steps a merchant or shopper would actually take.

When Eight moved to AI-assisted implementation, I reviewed generated code for quality and checked the resulting behaviour against the requirements.
